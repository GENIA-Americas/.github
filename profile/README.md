

## AI Learning Resources

GENIA-Americas recommends the following open AI resource collections:

- [Awesome Artificial Intelligence](https://github.com/owainlewis/awesome-artificial-intelligence)
  - A community-curated collection of AI courses, books, papers, and lectures.
  - Created by Owain Lewis, licensed under MIT.
  - We link to this resource as part of our commitment to AI education and knowledge sharing.


- [Awesome Spanish NLP](https://github.com/dav009/awesome-spanish-nlp)
  - A curated collection of linguistic resources for Spanish NLP and computational linguistics.
  - Created by David Przybilla, released under CC0 (public domain).
  - We link to this resource to support AI development that performs well on regional language data.


## Governance & Guidelines

- [Governance](https://github.com/GENIA-Americas/governance)
  - Our policies on opening new repositories, licensing defaults, and contribution guidelines.
- [Awesome Responsible AI](https://github.com/AthenaCore/AwesomeResponsibleAI)
  - A curated collection of frameworks, regulations, institutes, and standards for Responsible and Trustworthy AI.
  - Maintained by AthenaCore, licensed under MIT.
- [Awesome Production Machine Learning](https://github.com/EthicalML/awesome-production-machine-learning)
  - A curated collection of open source tools for deploying, monitoring, and securing production machine learning, including explainability, fairness, and privacy tooling.
  - Maintained by EthicalML, licensed under MIT.
